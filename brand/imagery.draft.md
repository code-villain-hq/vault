# Imagery — draft proposal

Status: visual mock for discussion. This is not an adopted brand rule.
See the [interactive study](index.html#imagery).

## Show one task

Choose a product view that explains something: an issue and its linked
specification, an agent’s work, or a launcher result. Lead with a real
capture when one is available. Illustrations can explain a concept while
captures are unavailable; label them as illustrations with example content.

## Capture honestly

- Use the actual interface and its colours. Studio framing stays Nightshade night; a capture can show a supported app theme.
- Prepare a sample workspace or hide private information before capture.
- Preserve useful context: the app or view name, the task, and its result.
- Do not invent product features or present an illustration as a screenshot.

## Frame quietly

- Start straight on, without perspective tilt, glow, or a decorative device frame.
- Use a neutral `panel` or `surface` ground. Begin with 24px of surround; reduce to 16px on narrow screens.
- Use the existing 10px image corner radius. Preserve native geometry inside the capture.
- Keep the capture’s aspect ratio for a whole view. Crop only to focus on a feature; do not stretch the image or cut away its meaning.
- Show the whole view for orientation and a detail crop when small text carries the point.

## Caption plainly

Place a caption 8px below the image in the `small` role. Name what the
image demonstrates. Say “Interface illustration · example content” when
it is a reconstruction. Give informative images useful alternative text.

## Proposed first use

The site’s existing interface illustrations remain in place. Replace them
with real captures later, using these same framing and caption decisions.
