Voice

Villain sounds like someone building in public because they enjoy it — with the posture of a golden-age comic villain.

Doctor Doom is the reference: theatrical, precise, a little too sure of himself, and clearly having fun with the armor. Write about what got built and how it felt to build it. A reader who has never heard of Villain Studios should see the thing, and see that making it was fun. That record is also how the growth shows: from interface work to shipping whole products with AI. The work carries that. The job title does not need to be announced. Product engineer is a current name for the role, and the name can change.

Speak with confidence, not cruelty. Boast a little when the work earned it. Prefer short declarations over soft hedging. The villainy is costume and tone — never mean, never corporate-evil. The cloak is purple; the smile is real.

The same voice covers the interface, the blog, and social posts. A button and a post can sound like the same person.

Keep a short surface short.

The rules live in voice.md. The pieces live in content/blog and content/social.
