Voice

Code Villain writes like a golden-age comic villain who ships.

Doctor Doom is the reference: theatrical, precise, a little too sure of himself. The armor is costume. The work is real.

Code Villain speaks, and names itself the way Doom does. “Code Villain shipped Forge.” Never “I”, never “we”.

Lead with the thing: what got built, what it does, what it cost. The reader sees the work before they hear the voice. Declare instead of hedging. Boast only what shipped. Name the number, the file, the trade-off. One flourish per piece; the rest is plain. Never mean, never corporate-evil. The villainy is posture.

The same voice covers the interface, the blog, and social posts. A button and a post sound like the same studio.

Short surfaces stay short.

The rules live in voice.md. The pieces live in content/blog and content/social.

Mark

The strike. A gothic C and V, Code Villain’s initials, with a poison bar behind them, cut where the letters cross: the homepage’s strike, made into a mark. It is the surface’s one brand moment. App icons are each app’s initial built the same way. Written in the mark folder.

Clear space: a quarter of the mark’s height on every side. Never recolour the bar, move it, tilt it, or put it over the letter.

Imagery

Draft

Show the work at a useful size. A capture should explain one task, with enough context to understand it.

A whole window establishes where the work happens.

Let the product provide the detail.

Capture
Real product captures come first. Show one task, hide private information before capture, and preserve the interface’s actual colours.
Present
Use a straight view on a neutral ground. Start with 24px of surround and 10px corners. Crop to explain a feature; keep its title and enough context. No perspective tilt or decorative glow.
Describe
Put a plain caption 8px below the image. Say what it proves. Label illustrations and example data; never pass them off as screenshots.

Draft wording: imagery.draft.md. The example above reuses the site’s interface illustration; real captures can replace it later.

Padding and groups. Begin with 16px in app panels, 24px around imagery, and 32px for a studio composition.

Separate major sections. Begin with 64px on studio pages, reduce to 48px on narrow screens. Reserve 96px for a genuine change of subject.
