Confidence chip

The coloured badge next to each language (e.g. 95%) is the LLM's own self-reported confidence in its translation — not an independent quality check. Colour reflects how the score compares to this pair's review threshold (configured per pair under Settings → Language Pairs).

  • ≥ thr At or above the review threshold — model is confident.
  • 50–thr Below threshold but still plausible — flagged for review.
  • < 50% Model itself thinks this is likely wrong — review strongly recommended.
  • human Edited or reviewed by a person — no automatic score applied.
