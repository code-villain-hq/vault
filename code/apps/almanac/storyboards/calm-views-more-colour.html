Calm views, more colour

Switching how a collection is drawn jumps: the block changes width, moves to another row, and the button you clicked slides away. Several views look alike in grey. Below: what happens today, a calmer switch, and colour for what a page keeps.

1 Switching stays put

Try View as in both (hover the block's title line). Same data, from Karriere.

Today · Board and Cards take the whole row, so the block drops below its neighbour, and the new picture snaps in.

Proposed · the block keeps its place and width; the height glides, the new picture fades in, View as stays put with the view's name and icon.

  • Keeps its width. Picking a view keeps the block as wide as it is; ⋯ still makes it the whole page. The app's own width still applies to blocks nobody switched.
  • Glides, 200 ms. Height eases to the new picture while it fades in (140 ms). None of it with Reduce motion on.
  • Says which. Each view has an icon; the one showing carries its name and the selected tint, and View as stays visible a moment after a click.
  • Principles: show less (icons, one name), small details (nothing moves under the pointer).

2 More colour, for what a page keeps

Six hues, the ones Nightshade already uses for code: one lightness each mode, so none shouts (even colour). Content wears them; the frame, titles and buttons stay grey.

Today

Proposed · Karriere's hue is the first; a choice's options take the next ones in their order.

Each group a hue · its topics wear it: on the topic's icon, in the sidebar and on the page.

  • A topic's hue draws its bars, lines, step numbers, list dots and big figures, and tints its icon.
  • A choice's options each get a hue in their order: bars, board lanes, pills. Where a choice colours, the topic's hue steps aside.
  • Status keeps its meaning. A status (open, under way, done) still shows grey, blue, teal. Today choices borrow those status colours as decoration, which principle 6 rules out; this replaces them.
  • Chrome dims, content leads: titles, View as, menus and the sidebar's words stay grey.

3 What it changes beyond Almanac

  • studio/design first. Principle 6 says cloak and poison are the only brand colours and greys stay grey. Data hues aren't brand colours, but they're new: a line in principles.md and six --data-* tokens built from syntax.json, so other apps can use them (Forge labels, Raven charts).
  • Two hues sit near status ones. Amber and teal come from the warn and ok seeds, so they're last in the order. A board of a choice with five options or more would show them.
