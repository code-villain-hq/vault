Starting a topic

How a topic begins: you name it and say what it sticks to, it opens empty, and a talk starts to fill it. Then Karl Marx a month later, and the Calendar that gathers every topic's dates. Night and day, with the Nightshade tokens. The content is made up.

1You name it and say what it sticks to

Sunday, Sep 27. New topic, or C, opens a dialog over the topic you're in. It has two fields: the name, and a sentence or two on what the topic sticks to, like a topic bot in Grok.

  • My choice: what it sticks to can stay empty. Almanac then writes it from the name and shows it on the topic, where you can change it.
  • My choice: Almanac picks the icon and the group. Karl Marx goes into red and green, next to Vegan. You can change both later.
  • My choice: the topic goes into the workspace you're in. ⌘↩ starts it.
