Less text on the page

Katzen keeps every point whole on its page: up to four lines each, four to a card, and a foot under every card. That reads like a document. The page is for a glance, so a point gets one line there; a section's page keeps it whole.

1A line per point

  • A point is one line on the page. It stops at its first semicolon, since what follows says why or how, and at the line's end, with "…". The whole point is on its section's page, and shows when you hover over it.
  • A card or tile shows three points at most, then "1 more", which opens the section. Cards show four today.
  • A step shows its name and one line.
  • The foot goes: no "4 points" or "Last kept 2 days ago". Only "1 more" stays, when a card leaves points out.
  • "You said" leaves the page. The section's page still says where each point came from. This drops the Oct 28 rule that kept "You said" on the page.
  • Chips, the week and lists stay as they are: they're one line already.
