Topics made of blocks

No page and no shape to pick: a topic is its blocks, in order. Haare, Pflanzen and Marx below are made of the same few blocks, so each topic gets its own layout and they still look like one app. Only the middle of the window, night only.

  • Seven blocks: Today (steps to tick off), Week, Chips, Shelf, Cards, Text and List. A block's title is the topic's own: Chips are "You" in Haare and "Terms" in Marx.
  • A template is the blocks a topic starts with. Almanac picks one when it sets the topic up, or you pick it. After that the blocks are the topic's.
  • Claude keeps each point in a block, and adds a block when you ask for one ("keep what I need to buy"), saying so in the talk.
  • ⋯ beside a block's title, on hover: Move up, Move down, Rename, Remove. Nothing else to arrange.

What it says

Where profit comes from

For Marx, a worker is paid for their labour power, but works longer than it takes to make that wage back. What's made in the rest of the day belongs to the owner: that is surplus value, and profit comes out of it. Das Kapital, Band 1, Kapitel 7

Why a coat is worth twenty yards of linen

Chapter 1 sets two goods side by side and asks what they share. Not their use, which differs, but the labour it took on average to make them. Money comes later, as the good every other is measured in. From the talk on Oct 22
