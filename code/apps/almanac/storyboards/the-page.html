The page, three ways

Haar-Routine as it is today, laid out three ways: only the middle of the window, night only. The points are the page's own, as short as the new keeping writes them. In all three the week is the only place reminders show: the reminder rows inside the Scalp card go.

1 + 2The routine, then you, your shelf, the advice

  • The steps and the week from 1, then the facts, the shelf and the advice from 2. Nothing is folded under a theme any more.
  • A shelf item's days come from its reminders; Apoliva has none, so it says how it's used instead.

1The routine leads

  • Lead with the action the state needs: on a wash day, the steps, in order, ticked off as you go. On other days, the next wash day's.
  • Show less: each theme is one line, what it says in a few words, with its points folded under it. One is open here.
  • It's the storyboard's Haare. Every topic would need its own "what leads", which Almanac picks with the shape.

2You, your shelf, the advice

  • Hierarchy by kind, not by theme: what's true of you is a row of facts, what you use is a shelf, and what to do is a short list. Today all three are mixed in every card.
  • Each bottle says when and how you use it; what's in it (ingredients, links) is behind its "notes".
  • Tone, not lines: facts are chips, bottles are surfaces, advice is plain text with the source dimmed.
