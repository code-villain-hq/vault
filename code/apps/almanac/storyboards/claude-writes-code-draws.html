3 Who decides what

Claude

  • What's true, with its source, as now.
  • Where it goes: which section, which thing.
  • What a line is: a field of a thing, a step in order, a day, a number, done, needs a look.
  • When you ask to tidy up: merge, split, rename, move. Never the look.

Code

  • The view, by fixed rules: things with the same fields are cards or a table, steps a checklist, short facts chips, sentences a list, days a timeline, days with numbers a trend.
  • The widths: it fills the rows.
  • The order: what's due and what needs a look first.

You

  • View as on any section: table, cards, list, timeline.
  • Your choice stays. Claude never changes it.
  • Nothing else to keep tidy.
