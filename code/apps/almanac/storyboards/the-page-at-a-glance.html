The page at a glance

Karriere, Geschichte, Pflanzen and Katzen all read as walls of sentences: each section's first paragraph in full, a source after every line, and a card per thing even when there is one thing. The page is for a glance; a section's page is for reading. Each block shows the shape of what it keeps: a tile per section, a face per card, a bar per number, a question to rehearse, a box to tick.

1Geschichte: a section is a tile

  • What it says shows each section as a tile: its name, its first sentence, when it happened and how many points it keeps. The tiles take the window's width; the paragraphs stay on the section's page, which its name opens. Ten sections fit on one screen, not ten screens.
  • Sources leave the page. "From Grok" under every paragraph says what every paragraph shares. The section's page keeps each source; the page shows only what you said and links.
  • In Zahlen, a Bars block: the figures the sections give, as bars on one scale per set, with the source under it. A bar opens the section it came from. Laid out by a talk ("zeig die Zahlen"), not by itself.
  • Reading gets ticks, as decided: what you've read stays, ticked, under what you haven't.
  • Terms keeps terms. "Lehnt alle Staaten ab" and "Zählt die USA geschichtlich zu Europa" are your views: they move to About. The Intifada figure moves to In Zahlen.

2Pflanzen: a card has a face

  • A card shows a short line per fact, each with its icon: light, water, place, cutting. The block names the facts its cards have, and Claude keeps each point under one; the sentences stay on the plant's page.
  • The state shows only when it needs a look: the Korbmarante's brown edges and the Calathea's fungus gnats in warn, the cutting ready to pot in info. A plant that's fine says nothing.
  • Water says when: the next Gießtag from the Week block, on the cards it waters.
  • A photo takes the icon's place once a talk has one of the plant. Pflanzen's talks have none yet: its points came over from Grok.
  • The foot counts what's kept ("7 points") in place of "Last kept today" on every card.

3Katzen: one card is a profile

  • Cards with a single card show it as a profile: Lisa's picture, her name, and what you'd say first about her as chips: age, weight, breed, coat, and that she's vaccinated, chipped and spayed.
  • Her 47 points go into tiles by what they're about: Futter, Spielen und Schlafen, Streicheln, Mit der Tochter, Draußen, Gesundheit, Wesen. Each tile shows its three most useful lines; its name opens the rest.
  • No new block: it's how Cards looks with one card. A second cat turns it back into cards, with these tiles inside each one's page.
  • Her reminder stays at the top of her profile, as it sits on her card today.

4Karriere: questions, bars and ticks

  • Questions, a new block, to rehearse: a question a row, who asked it, and its answer folded under it, taken from what the page keeps. The tick says you've practised it. Frag mich ab starts a talk that asks them one by one and ticks what you answered well. A question with no answer yet says so.
  • Bars, a new block: what the market wants, a bar per skill across the 28 ads, with what you show for it. A gap is in warn. A bar opens the ads that asked for it, so the companies are the evidence, not the page.
  • Lücken schließen, a list with ticks: the gaps written as paragraphs today, as things to do, each with the section it came from.
  • Trends and the CV stay text, as tiles. The ads stay cards, under the fold; Profil is what Karriere knows about you, so it goes to About, which still shows it whole.

5What changes for Claude

  • Two new blocks, Questions and Bars, each described to the layout as what it's for: questions you'd be asked, numbers to compare. Without that, a layout keeps choosing Text, as Karriere's did.
  • A Cards block names its facts (Licht, Wasser, Platz, Schneiden), and each point carries the fact it's about. A point that fits none stays a line on the plant's page.
  • A section has a first sentence that stands alone: the bold lead and the sentence it starts, which the tile shows.
  • Facts about you go to You, however a talk words them: your views, your profile, your hair.
  • My order to build: tiles and sources once (1), card faces (2), the profile (3), then Bars, Questions and ticks.
