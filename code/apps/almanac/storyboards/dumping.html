Dumping something

What happens when you just need to get something out of your head: where it goes, and what you see afterwards. Tuesday, Oct 27. Night and day, with the Nightshade tokens. The content is made up.

1You dump it from wherever you are

Tuesday, 14:02, at work, in the middle of something else. A shortcut opens one field over whatever you're doing. You type or paste and keep it. There's no topic to pick.

  • My choice: the shortcut works anywhere on the Mac. The app doesn't have to be open in front.
  • My choice: the dump goes into the workspace the panel shows, the one you used last. You switch it before you keep.

2It says where it went

Return, and the panel names the topic it went to, and where in it. There's something to do in it, so it offers a reminder, as agreed: the app decides when.

  • My choice: Open goes to the topic; Undo takes the dump back out.
  • My choice: typing again starts the next dump. Esc closes the panel.

3When it isn't sure, it asks right there

Two dumps on the same day. The first fits two topics in Work. The second fits no topic in Home yet.

  • My choice: one tap answers. Both keeps it in each topic.
  • My choice: if you close it without answering, it still goes somewhere. A dump that fits two goes to the likelier one, and the question waits there, like in Manage up. A dump that fits none starts the topic it proposed, which you can rename or remove.

4Later, it's where you'd look

Tuesday evening, at home. The dump sits on Mochi's card with the unread dot, and Friday's reminder is on the week. Zähne, started from the second dump, carries the dot in the sidebar.

  • My choice: a dump is kept like a talk. The card counts both, and the dump opens from there.
  • My choice: the new part carries the unread dot until you've seen it, as in Manage up.
